# Business Questions

The project was structured around the following business questions to guide the analysis and dashboard development.

| BQ | Business Question | Status | Dashboard | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BQ-01 | How do restaurant ratings vary across cities and states? | ☑ | 1. Executive Overview | High |
| BQ-02 | Which restaurants have the highest overall ratings? | ☑ | 2. Restaurant Intelligence | High |
| BQ-03 | How do restaurant policies and settings relate to overall ratings? | ☑ | 2. Restaurant Intelligence | High |
| BQ-04 | How is restaurant cuisine supply distributed across different cuisines? | ☑ | 2. Restaurant Intelligence | Medium |
| BQ-05 | What are the dominant consumer demographics, behaviors, and spending patterns? | ☑ | 3. Consumer Intelligence | Very High |
| BQ-06 | Which cuisines are most preferred by consumers? | ☑ | 3. Consumer Intelligence | High |
| BQ-07 | Where do consumer demand and restaurant supply differ by cuisine? | ☑ | 4. Market Gap Analysis | Very High |
| BQ-08 | Which cuisines show both market gaps and potential rating concerns? | ☑ | 4. Market Gap Analysis | High |

## Dashboard Mapping

### Dashboard 1 — Executive Overview

- **BQ-01:** How do restaurant ratings vary across cities and states?

### Dashboard 2 — Restaurant Intelligence

- **BQ-02:** Which restaurants have the highest overall ratings?
- **BQ-03:** How do restaurant policies and settings relate to overall ratings?
- **BQ-04:** How is restaurant cuisine supply distributed across different cuisines?

### Dashboard 3 — Consumer Intelligence

- **BQ-05:** What are the dominant consumer demographics, behaviors, and spending patterns?
- **BQ-06:** Which cuisines are most preferred by consumers?

### Dashboard 4 — Market Gap Analysis

- **BQ-07:** Where do consumer demand and restaurant supply differ by cuisine?
- **BQ-08:** Which cuisines show both market gaps and potential rating concerns?

## Analysis Objective

These business questions were used to structure the PivotTable analysis, KPI calculations, and four interactive Excel dashboards. The objective was to move from raw restaurant and consumer data toward insights related to restaurant performance, consumer behavior, cuisine preferences, and potential market gaps.
